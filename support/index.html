遇到问题或有建议

常见问题

  • 需要账号或网络吗? 不需要。学习、查词和发音都在本机完成。
  • 学习记录在哪里? 只保存在你的 iPhone 上。删除 App 会同时删除记录。

隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

RootType Support

RootType is an offline English vocabulary typing app for iPhone (iOS 17 or later).

Questions and feedback

Please leave a message on the public issues page with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. Please do not post sensitive personal information.

FAQ

  • Do I need an account or internet? No. Learning, lookup and pronunciation all happen on your device.
  • Where is my progress stored? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.
