EngType 支持

EngType 是一款英语打字背单词 App,支持 iPhone 和 iPad(iOS 17 及以上)。词书、词典和练习内容都在本机,登录 iCloud 后学习进度会在你的设备之间同步。

遇到问题或有建议

请在公开问题页留言,写明设备型号(iPhone 或 iPad)、系统版本和问题经过。请不要填写个人敏感信息。

常见问题

  • 需要账号或网络吗? 不需要注册账号。学习、查词和发音都在本机完成,不联网也能用;联网时学习进度通过 iCloud 同步,购买和恢复购买需要连接 App Store。
  • 学习记录在哪里? 保存在你的设备上;登录 iCloud 时同时保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库,用于在 iPhone 和 iPad 之间同步,开发者无法查看。
  • 为什么另一台设备没有同步? 请确认两台设备登录同一个 Apple ID,并在系统“设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud”中允许 EngType。同步状态可在 App 的“设置 → 关于 EngType”中查看。

隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

EngType Support

EngType is an English vocabulary typing app for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later). Word books, the dictionary and practice content are on the device; when signed in to iCloud, learning progress syncs between your devices.

Questions and feedback

Please leave a message on the public issues page with your device model (iPhone or iPad), iOS version and what happened. Please do not post sensitive personal information.

FAQ

  • Do I need an account or internet? No account. Learning, lookup and pronunciation work on the device without a connection; when online, progress syncs through iCloud, and purchases and restores need the App Store.
  • Where is my progress stored? On your device and, when signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud database so it syncs between iPhone and iPad. The developer cannot see it.
  • Why isn't my other device syncing? Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID and EngType is allowed under Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Sync status is shown in the app under Settings → About EngType.
