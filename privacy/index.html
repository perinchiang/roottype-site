EngType 隐私政策

生效日期:2026 年 10 月 26 日 · 适用版本:EngType 1.0 及以后(iPhone 与 iPad)

简要说明

EngType 是一款英语打字背单词 App。它不需要注册 EngType 账号,不包含广告、统计或第三方 SDK,不出售、不共享你的任何数据。你的学习进度保存在你的设备上;如果设备登录了 iCloud,还会通过你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库在你的 iPhone 和 iPad 之间同步。开发者无法查看这些数据。

保存在你设备上的内容

  • 学习进度、复习记录、默写与听写记录、学习时长和学习记录(热力图)、收藏的单词,以及你选择的设置(主题、深浅色、音效、触觉反馈、提示方式、每次学习和复习的词数)。
  • 这些内容保存在 App 的专用存储区和系统偏好设置中。删除 App 会同时删除设备上的这些数据。
  • 如果你开启了 iCloud 备份或电脑备份,这些本地文件可能按系统规则被包含在设备备份中。这由你的系统设置和 Apple 的条款管理,不经过开发者。

iCloud 同步

  • 设备登录 iCloud 时,EngType 会把学习进度、复习记录、学习记录和收藏保存到 Apple 为你提供的 CloudKit 私有数据库,用于在你登录同一 Apple ID 的设备之间同步。
  • 这些数据只属于你的 iCloud 账户,存放在 Apple 的服务器上,受 Apple 隐私政策约束。开发者无法读取、导出或删除其中任何一条记录。
  • 同步的内容只包含上面列出的学习数据,不包含你的姓名、邮箱、联系人或其他个人信息。每台设备会生成一个随机编号来区分各自的记录,这个编号与你的身份无关。
  • 未登录 iCloud,或在系统“设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud”中关闭 EngType 时,App 功能完全相同,只是不同步,数据只保存在本机。
  • 如需删除 iCloud 中的 EngType 数据,可在系统“设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间”中删除 EngType 的数据。

购买

一次性买断由 Apple App Store 处理。开发者不会收到你的付款信息,只会从 Apple 获得“是否已购买”的结果,用于在设备上解锁内容。

我们不收集什么

EngType 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、联系人、照片、麦克风或摄像头内容、广告标识符、使用统计、崩溃日志,也不会跟踪你。App 不请求定位、相机、麦克风等系统权限。

发音与词典

单词发音使用随 App 打包的录音,没有录音时使用设备自带的英语语音合成。词典和词表随 App 一起安装,查词不需要网络,也不会向任何服务器发送你输入的内容。

外部链接

App 的“关于”页提供本政策和支持页的链接。你点击后,系统会用 Safari 打开网页,浏览器会访问托管本页的 GitHub Pages,GitHub 可能按其隐私声明记录访问信息(例如 IP 地址)。EngType 本身不会向其发送任何数据。

儿童

EngType 不向开发者收集任何人的数据,包括儿童。

政策变更

如果以后的版本改变了数据处理方式,我们会先更新本政策并在 App 内说明,再发布该版本。

联系方式

隐私问题请在公开问题页留言。请不要在其中填写个人敏感信息。

EngType Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026 · Applies to EngType 1.0 and later (iPhone and iPad)

Summary

EngType is an English vocabulary typing app. It needs no EngType account and contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs. It does not sell or share any data. Your learning progress is stored on your device and, if the device is signed in to iCloud, synced between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot see this data.

What stays on your device

  • Learning progress, review records, dictation and recall records, study time and activity history (heatmap), favorite words, and your settings (theme, light/dark, sounds, haptics, hint style, learning and review batch sizes).
  • This information is stored in the app's own storage and system preferences. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, these local files may be included in your device backup under Apple's rules. The developer does not receive them.

iCloud sync

  • When the device is signed in to iCloud, EngType saves learning progress, review records, activity history and favorites to your CloudKit private database, provided by Apple, so they sync between devices signed in to the same Apple ID.
  • This data belongs to your iCloud account and is stored on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot read, export or delete any of it.
  • Only the learning data listed above is synced: no name, email, contacts or other personal information. Each device creates a random identifier to tell its records apart; it is not linked to your identity.
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn off EngType under Settings → [your name] → iCloud, the app works exactly the same without syncing, and data stays on the device.
  • To delete EngType data from iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete EngType's data.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed by the Apple App Store. The developer never receives your payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase was made, to unlock content on your device.

What we do not collect

EngType does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera content, advertising identifiers, usage analytics or crash logs, and does not track you. The app does not request location, camera, microphone or similar permissions.

Pronunciation and dictionary

Word pronunciations use recordings bundled with the app, or the device's English speech voice when no recording exists. The dictionary and word list are installed with the app. Looking up a word needs no network and sends nothing anywhere.

External links

The app's About screen links to this policy and the support page. If you tap a link, iOS opens it in Safari, which contacts GitHub Pages, the host of these pages. GitHub may log visit information such as your IP address under its own privacy statement. EngType itself sends no data.

Children

EngType collects no data from anyone, including children, for the developer.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and explained in the app before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, please leave a message on the public issues page. Please do not post sensitive personal information there.
