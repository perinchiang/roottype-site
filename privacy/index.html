RootType 隐私政策

生效日期:2026 年 10 月 16 日 · 适用版本:RootType 1.0

简要说明

RootType 是一款离线的英语单词输入与记忆 App。它不需要账号,不联网,不收集、不上传、不出售、不共享你的任何个人数据,也不包含广告、统计或第三方 SDK。

保存在你设备上的内容

  • 学习进度、默写与听写记录、学习时长和日历记录,以及你选择的设置(外观、音效、触觉反馈、自动发音、轻提示、每次练习词数)。
  • 这些内容只保存在你的 iPhone 本地(App 的专用存储区和系统偏好设置中),开发者无法访问。
  • 如果你开启了 iCloud 备份或电脑备份,这些本地文件可能按系统的备份规则被包含在 Apple 提供的设备备份中。这由你的系统设置和 Apple 的条款管理,不经过开发者。
  • 删除 App 会同时删除这些数据。

我们不收集什么

RootType 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、联系人、照片、麦克风或摄像头内容、设备标识符、广告标识符、使用统计、崩溃日志或任何其他数据。App 不请求任何系统权限,也不会跟踪你。

发音与词典

单词发音使用随 App 打包的录音,没有录音时使用 iPhone 自带的英语语音合成。词典和词表随 App 一起安装,查词不需要网络,也不会向任何服务器发送你输入的内容。

外部链接

App 的“关于”页提供本政策的链接。你点击后,系统会用 Safari 打开本页。此时你的浏览器会访问托管本页的 GitHub Pages,GitHub 可能按其隐私声明记录访问信息(例如 IP 地址),RootType 本身不会向其发送任何数据。不点击链接则没有任何网络访问。

儿童

RootType 不收集任何人的数据,包括儿童。

政策变更

如果以后的版本改变了数据处理方式(例如加入账号或云同步),我们会先更新本政策并在 App 内说明,再发布该版本。

联系方式

隐私问题请在公开问题页留言。请不要在其中填写个人敏感信息。

RootType Privacy Policy

Effective: October 16, 2026 · Applies to RootType 1.0

Summary

RootType is an offline English vocabulary typing app. It requires no account, makes no network connections, and does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Learning progress, dictation and recall records, study time and calendar history, and your settings (appearance, sounds, haptics, auto-pronunciation, hints, batch sizes).
  • This information is stored locally on your iPhone and is not accessible to the developer.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, these local files may be included in your device backup under Apple's rules and your system settings. The developer does not receive them.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data.

What we do not collect

RootType does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera content, device or advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash logs, or any other data. The app requests no system permissions and does not track you.

Pronunciation and dictionary

Word pronunciations use recordings bundled with the app, or the on-device iOS speech voice when no recording exists. The dictionary and word list are installed with the app. Looking up a word needs no network and sends nothing anywhere.

External link

The app's About screen links to this policy. If you tap the link, iOS opens this page in Safari. Your browser then contacts GitHub Pages, which hosts this page, and GitHub may log visit information such as your IP address under its own privacy statement. RootType itself sends no data. If you do not tap the link, no network access occurs.

Children

RootType collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example accounts or cloud sync), this policy will be updated and explained in the app before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, please leave a message on the public issues page. Please do not post sensitive personal information there.
